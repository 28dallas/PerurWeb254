# Maintenance and Support Plan

## Support Scope
- Bug fixes for existing features
- Content rendering issues
- Minor UX adjustments
- Integration troubleshooting (forms, newsletter, CMS data)

## Support Window
- Hypercare: first 30 days after launch
- Business hours response SLA:
- Critical issues: within 4 hours
- High priority: within 1 business day
- Normal priority: within 3 business days

## Change Categories
- Minor changes: text, styling tweaks, CMS field updates
- Standard changes: page/module updates with low risk
- Major changes: architecture or integration changes (scheduled release)

## Update Procedures
- All fixes in feature branch
- Lint and typecheck before merge
- Deploy via controlled release
- Post-deploy smoke test on key pages and forms

## Escalation
- Level 1: content editor or communications lead
- Level 2: technical maintainer
- Level 3: engineering escalation for production incidents
