# Staff Sanity Access Sheet

## Sanity Workspace Details
- Project name: `Perur Rays of Hope`
- Project ID: `jyy11ruo`
- Dataset: `production`
- Local Studio command: `npm run studio`

## What Staff Need To Log In
- Their own email address
- An invitation to the Sanity project
- The correct role in Sanity
- The Studio link from the technical maintainer

## Recommended Access Roles

### Communications Lead
- Role: Editor
- Can update: Homepage, Blog Posts, Events, Testimonials, Site Settings

### Content Editor
- Role: Editor
- Can update: Programs, Resources, Team Members, About Page, Homepage

### Technical Maintainer
- Role: Administrator
- Can update: All content
- Also manages: project access, schema changes, deployment, and troubleshooting

## Login Steps For Staff
1. Receive an email invitation to the Sanity project.
2. Accept the invitation using the same email address.
3. Open the Studio link shared by the technical maintainer.
4. Sign in with the invited email account.
5. Open the content type you need and begin editing.

## How To Invite Staff
1. Sign in to the Sanity project admin area.
2. Open project members or roles.
3. Invite the staff member using their work email.
4. Assign the correct role.
5. Ask them to accept the invite before training begins.

## What Each Staff Member Should Edit
- `Homepage`: hero section, vision, mission, impact stats, stories heading, CTA section
- `Blog Post`: news, updates, articles
- `Event`: upcoming events and dates
- `Program`: program summaries, activities, funding details
- `Resource`: reports, PDFs, policies, downloadable files
- `Team Member`: names, roles, bios, photos
- `Testimonial`: quotes shown on the homepage
- `Site Settings`: email, phone, address, social links

## What To Share With Staff
- Their invited email address
- Their role
- The Studio link
- The short training guide in [TRAINING_HANDOVER_GUIDE.md](/home/nathan/Documents/Perur%20WEB/docs/TRAINING_HANDOVER_GUIDE.md)

## Important Notes
- Do not share one common login among all staff
- Each person should use their own invited email account
- Only administrators should manage permissions or technical settings
- Editors should publish only after checking spelling, dates, links, and images
